## Frame 1 — The alert

- scene: Credit-alert card punches in cold on the ₦ figure, nothing else
- duration: 4s
- poster: 3.2s
- transition_in: none
- status: built
- src: compositions/frames/01-alert.html
- blueprint: `kinetic-type-beats` (hook role) · rules `spring-pop-entrance`, `counting-dynamic-scale`

Cold open, 0:00. A credit alert on the chalk grid, framed by the two hairlines,
in the left column — the third of the frame a real alert would occupy. Only the
alert card enters — no headline, no brand, no field (no front-loading). The
figure reveals across the back half of the beat: `₦1,240,000` counts up on
`money-hero` (14cqh) at `9cqw / 41.5cqh`, then the meta row `Credited · 10 Oct ·
09:41` lands, then the hook line in outcome language, `Gone by the 15th.`, in the
right column at 40cqh. The empty middle is the beat — the money on one side, the
punchline on the other, the letterbox between them.

Narrative role: state the pain in the viewer's own language, in one breath.
Traced to the message: the vanishing is what the message denies.

## Frame 2 — Five hands

- scene: Labels close in from every edge, the figure shrinks as each is subtracted
- duration: 6.5s
- poster: 6.0s
- transition_in: cut
- status: built
- src: compositions/frames/02-grab.html
- blueprint: `overwhelm-surround` · rules `reactive-displacement`, `counting-dynamic-scale`, `spring-pop-entrance`

The alert survives as a chrome strip up top and the figure holds frame 1's
exact slot — `9cqw / 41.5cqh / 14cqh` — so the cut drops the card and the number
does not move. Five labels arrive from the edges — `Rent ₦450,000`,
`Debt ₦180,000`, `Family ₦150,000`, `Friday ₦80,000`, `Data ₦25,000`. Each
arrival drives the remaining figure down and its scale with it: ₦1,240,000 →
₦355,000. The subtraction is felt as weight, not arithmetic, because one driver
per grab derives both the hand's arrival and the amount's loss.

The five hands are one system, not scatter: identical 16cqw blocks, same
padding, snapped to the chalk grid — a band across the lower half on two
alternating baselines (60cqh / 70cqh) so they read as a stagger, not a crooked
line. Arrival order is the biggest loss first, so the shrink accelerates the
read. The disorder is timed in the build pass (stagger, arrival direction,
overshoot); it is never drawn into the layout, because a crooked block reads as
carelessness, not as pressure.

At 4.9s the claim that the whole video pays off prints in `statement`, in frame
1's hook slot — the answer where the question was: `Every naira has a position.`
The promise lands on beat two.

Narrative role: raise the stakes, then land the value claim. Traced: this is
the loss the formation prevents.

The chaos dissolves into the field. Chalk strokes draw boundary, halfway line,

Narrative role: the first evidence — the claim becomes a shape. Traced: every
naira has a position, and here are eleven of them.

The field holds perfectly still at full bleed on the left — the same geometry as

`Pay · ₦1,240,000` / `Covered · ₦992,000` / `Unassigned · ₦0`

of 10000). `Unassigned ₦0` is the volt hit and the argument.

the pitch instead of across it, the keeper stays in the field and all eleven
positions remain readable. The panel clears the hairlines and the run bar.

Narrative role: proof, in the app's own arithmetic. Traced: the shape is not a
metaphor, it is the split.

## Structure notes

- Canvas is 2560x1080 (21:9), chosen for an "HD feel". Because the short edge
  governs legibility on a frame this wide, the type ramp and vertical rhythm are
  in `cqh` and horizontal placement in `cqw`. 1920x1080 is the same image
  scaled; nothing is repositioned for it.
- Six frames, six files, one visual track. Root `data-duration="30"`. Slots adjoin
  at 0 / 4 / 10.5 / 16.5 / 21 / 25.5.
- Frames 3, 4, and 5 each carry the pitch field in their own file, with
  identical geometry and coordinates, so the cut from the assembled shape into
  the ledger reads as one held shot rather than a redraw. If the build finds a
  seam, the fix is to merge 03-05 into one sub-composition with internal phase
  divs (composition-patterns archetype C) rather than to nudge the coordinates.
- Audio (ping, five impacts, one calm hit) mounts as a root-level `<audio>` at
  the assembly stage, never inside a frame file.
- `index.html` is now the orchestrator: six slots on one visual track, root
  timeline empty. Every frame file is a sub-composition, so each carries its own
  `@font-face` block — the runtime discards a sub-comp `<head>`, and ₦ (U+20A6)
  lives in `latin-ext`, so a latin-only face would render every currency sign in
  a fallback.
- Sketches are wireframes: real words, plain blocks, frame.md ground and ink,
  volt only on the focal read of each frame. Full treatment and motion arrive
  after the board is confirmed.
- Derivative canvases are recomposed, never cropped. A 1080-wide slice of this
  2560 frame cuts three of the five hands in half, so the 1:1 and 9:16 key
  graphics are their own layouts (`compositions/stills/`): the square keeps the
  block system and breaks the band three-over-two, the portrait turns the hands
  into the app's ledger row. A still also loses the run bar — progress is an
  artifact of the film.
- Sound is silent-first: no music bed, cues hand-placed as root-level
  `<audio>`, one lane each, because two clips on a track force the earlier one
  to end and would clip every decaying tail.
