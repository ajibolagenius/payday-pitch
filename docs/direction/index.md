## How to read their status

- **Recorded:** A founder answer reported in research §0.1, or a current project rule. Its source is stated. This does not approve every feature associated with it.
- **Proposed:** A report recommendation or a new recommendation in this synthesis. It needs a decision or evidence.
- **Open:** We cannot infer the founder's answer. No response means it stays open.

The existing [scope](../scope/scope.md), [specs](../specs/0001-stack-architecture/index.md), [design system](../../design.md) and [project rules](../../AGENTS.md) retain their authority. ## What we carry forward

Source: [research §0.1](../research/2026-10-26-unified-research-report.md#01-founder-decisions-already-on-record).

Current founder answers, 27 October 2026: the first recruitment pool is working friends, mostly Lagos, in remote and on site jobs. The founder's own three incomes arrive over the final three days of the month and are allocated as they arrive. Required and extra debt repayments come before predictable transport/data and uncertain food costs. The first useful job is seeing what remains after debt and essentials. This is one founder scenario, not yet evidence about all recruits.

Agreed product direction: four flexible lines, equivalent pitch/list views, acknowledged shortfall saving, separate received/forecast remainders, optional allocations reduced before essentials, weekly food checks and Keeper from the first plan with zero allowed. Plans are named for the month they cover. A sample precedes signup and personal details. Clean sheets count due essentials and required debt recorded as paid. The desired release includes the full loop, reminders, share cards, goals and celebrations. Cards reveal shape and percentages only. All four reminder moments are welcome; cadence needs testing. Basic planning and original records remain free. Future bank connected automation prepares a draft for review without moving money. A free two pay cycle pilot precedes a paid benefit test.

Agreed immediate milestone: clickable flow prototype and recruit first pilot users this week, at minimal cost. Three friends are candidates: a remote data analyst who supports Chelsea, an on site digital marketer who supports Manchester United, and a third person not yet described. None is recorded as contacted or enrolled. Matchday leads the creative study, with Floodlight also interesting. The identity should feel Nigerian with room to expand; a descriptor can explain the financial purpose. Final name, palette, type, spend ceiling and available hours remain open. The [interview record](validation-plan.md#interview-record) preserves the answers and history.

Current constraints: planning without custody or money movement; exact amounts; Lagos time; truthful payment status; accessible interaction; no shared caching of signed in money pages. Sources: [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the [current scope](../scope/scope.md).
