## User and promise

Recorded audience: Nigerian salary earners. The founder's initial recruitment pool is working friends, mostly in Lagos, including remote and on site workers with varying pay. **D01 is partially answered:** pay bands, payment regularity, football interest, current tools and specific participants remain unknown.

Founder selected first job: **See what remains after debt and essentials across all three incomes.** The broader proposed promise remains: know what this pay must cover, choose what the rest will do, and carry that plan through to your next payday. Football makes priorities visible and the return visit memorable.

Founder payday scenario, recorded 27 October 2026: three jobs pay over the final three days of the month. The founder usually allocates each payment as it arrives, expecting the others soon after. Debt repayment combines a required monthly amount with additional repayment chosen to clear the debt faster. The founder then sets aside a large part of the remainder for transport, food, data and miscellaneous spending. Transport and data are predictable to the founder; food is not. Little is left to save. We have not established whether this pattern also describes the recruitment pool.

Agreed on 27 October 2026: show the received pay remainder separately from a forecast, suggest reducing extra debt repayment or another optional allocation before essentials, and use a weekly food allowance with a quick remaining amount check. Neither remainder is presented as a bank balance. The founder has not yet chosen calendar month versus pay cycle boundaries or what a late payment does to the plan.

Proposed consequences still to test: one plan can account for several income arrivals; required debt payments are separate from any additional repayment the user chooses; the product remains useful in a month with little or no saving. Multiple income recording should be considered for the core experience rather than assumed to be a premium extra. These remain recommendations, not additional founder decisions.

The app plans and records. The user's bank or existing savings service keeps the money. A saved plan is not a payment, and the app does not recommend specific financial products.

**Entry experience agreed, 27 October 2026 (D12):** explore a clearly labelled sample plan first; personal financial details come after account creation. The prototype can simulate that account step. Do not request real credentials, imply a real account was created, or turn the sample into a guest draft containing personal money data. The earlier report recommendation to show value before signup is met through the sample.

## Agreed first release loop (D06)

The founder accepted this full loop on 27 October 2026, including the weekly food check. Reminders, share cards, dedicated goals and clean sheet celebrations are also requested for the first release to support gamification and user experience. Their presence is agreed; their exact behaviour and build order still need design. **This week's milestone is agreed:** a clickable flow prototype and recruiting the first pilot users, with minimal cost. That milestone does not remove the extras from the desired release or claim a public launch this week.

| Moment            | User result                                                                         | Completion evidence                                                      |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Prepare           | Enter take home pay, essentials, required debt payments and relevant obligations    | Expected and received income are visibly separate                        |
| Plan              | Assign confirmed money to real purposes and inspect what is uncovered               | Exact total, remainder and gaps are visible                              |
| Review and save   | Understand the trade offs and commit to a plan                                      | User can explain what happens next and that no money moved               |

| Review and return | Compare planned with recorded, identify missing information, prepare the next cycle | Carry forward is explicit and does not duplicate income or contributions |

**Keeper agreed, 27 October 2026 (part of D03):** include the emergency reserve from the first plan, allow a zero new contribution, and show an existing reserve balance separately from this month's contribution. The balance is manually reported, not verified. This does not imply the founder currently holds a reserve. Zero contribution is not a reason to fabricate savings or mark a user as failing.

The research's remaining v1 catalogue is a menu for prioritisation. **Proposed supporting requirements:** due dates and fixtures, late/partial salary and honest offline/error states. Basic corrections and access to original records are part of the agreed free boundary; free CSV is the proposed export route. If any agreed release item is to move later, return that trade off to the founder rather than changing the release cut by assumption.

**Sharing agreed:** formation shape and line percentages only, with amounts, personal purpose names and identity hidden. Preview before sharing. The clarification in chat distinguishes this from the alternative that would allow a card containing amounts; that alternative was not selected.

**Reminder moments agreed:** expected payday, an upcoming essential payment, weekly food check and month review. The founder welcomes all four. Exact frequency and delivery channels are not specified. Proposed prototype behaviour: user chosen reminder settings, no duplicate prompt for the same event, and an easy way to turn each off. Test cadence rather than treating tolerance of these moments as permission for repeated nudges.

Deeper coaching, scenarios, mini leagues, household planning, inflation views and elaborate season graphics remain later candidates. Consumer subscription is recorded business direction. The founder agrees to test one premium benefit and has identified bank connected allocation from saved preferences as something they would pay for, with no money movement. The premium interaction and payment sequence are captured below; the integration needs a later design.

## Formation meaning (D03)

**Four lines with flexible real purposes agreed, 27 October 2026 (D03).** The football formation explains the plan; there is no required count of eleven purposes. This is the chosen product direction. Updating the existing five zone scope and specs is a later handoff, not an implementation change in this task.

| Line     | Agreed financial meaning                                   | Detail to resolve                                                                                  |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Keeper   | Accessible emergency cash reserve                          | Agreed: show existing balance separately from this cycle's contribution; zero contribution allowed |
| Defence  | Essentials and required debt payments                      | Where do monthly provisions for annual essentials belong?                                          |
| Midfield | Flexible spending, family support and nearer term purposes | How do we protect family support that is genuinely non optional?                                   |
| Attack   | Longer term goals and wealth contributions                 | Is a course a near term commitment or a longer term goal in this user's situation?                 |

Family support may be protected as essential when appropriate; it is not always discretionary Midfield spending. A label such as family support or course cannot tell us by itself whether an expense is essential. The exact priority and due date properties of a purpose remain design questions.

Purposes are the user's actual labels and amounts. Do not invent eleven expenses to fill a formation. Presets are editable starting allocations with visible amounts and trade offs. A formation name never determines investment risk.

A separate spending Bench is not part of the agreed four line model. The report's optional parked goal Bench remains a later concept, not an approved feature. “Your XI” needs comprehension testing when the plan can contain any number of purposes. **D02 agreed, 27 October 2026:** keep the football identity and make pitch and list equally usable. Neither view should omit an action or change the underlying amounts.

## Money and time questions that must be answered

- **Shortfall (D04, agreed 27 October 2026):** Allow saving with a clear shortfall and explicit acknowledgement, including when the user allocates to Attack. Proposed review detail: identify affected commitments and never label an underfunded plan as fully covered. The priority model and exact review interaction still need specification.
- **Income and period (D05, agreed direction):** The founder allocates each salary as it arrives. Show both the received pay remainder and a separate forecast. Name the plan for the month the money must cover: pay arriving in late October to fund November bills belongs to the November plan in the agreed example. Preserve actual receipt dates rather than relabelling the payment date. Expected income is not received money. Late income treatment, allocation to a target month, both figures' inputs and exact cutoff rules still need specification.
- **Carry forward (D05):** Separate leftover planning allocation, money already held, and a new contribution. None should be counted twice.
- **Annual obligations (D03/D05):** Distinguish money put aside monthly from the eventual bill payment. Agree what happens when the bill is due before enough has accumulated.
- **Reserve (D03, agreed in part):** Show opening balance and new contribution separately, from the first plan. Contribution may be zero. A withdrawal is not automatically failure; its effect on history and progress remains open.
- **Debt and adjustment priority (D03, agreed direction):** Required debt payments belong with essentials. When an essential estimate rises, suggest reducing extra debt repayment or another optional allocation first; the user chooses the final change. Only an uncompleted allocation is available for reassignment. Distinguishing required and additional amounts in the data and their exact visual grouping needs specification.
- **Variable essentials (D03/D06, agreed interaction):** Use a weekly food allowance with a quick remaining amount check. Food remains essential even when its cost varies. Decide how the user updates the remaining amount, what starts a week, how leftover allowance carries forward and how a partial week fits a plan period. A lightweight weekly check does not by itself approve a detailed transaction ledger.
- **Corrections (D07):** Define how editing a past payment, changing a due date or retiring a commitment affects review and clean sheet history.

Worked scenario for discussion, using the report's illustrative totals: ₦600,000 received; ₦330,000 living costs, rent provision and required debt; ₦115,000 family and flexible spending; ₦80,000 reserve contribution; ₦75,000 course fund. Total: ₦600,000. Only ₦400,000 arrives. We must show the ₦200,000 gap and ask what changes. We cannot decide which obligation loses funding from its football label alone.

Additional founder based prototype scenario, with sample amounts to be chosen rather than personal amounts: three salaries arrive over several days; the first is allocated before the others arrive; debt has required and additional portions; transport and data estimates stay steady while weekly food needs rise. Show received and forecast remainders separately. Suggest reducing an uncompleted optional allocation before essentials. Test an adjustment both before and after an extra repayment has been recorded: a completed repayment cannot be treated as available cash or silently undone to fund food.

## Language and evidence

| Term           | Meaning                                                                              |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Expected       | A future income forecast                                                             |
| Planned        | A saved intention to allocate money                                                  |
| Recorded       | An action the user reports completing                                                |
| Verified       | Confirmed by a defined external source; unavailable in the manual first product      |
| Left to assign | Confirmed planning money without a purpose, not a bank balance                       |
| Clean sheet    | Agreed: all essentials and required debt payments due in the period recorded as paid |

**Clean sheet agreed, 27 October 2026 (D07):** all essentials and required debt payments due in the period are recorded as paid. Optional extra debt payments, savings contributions and a zero Keeper contribution do not disqualify a user. Missing records show “not enough information”; an empty plan earns no award. The exact eligible item and correction rules still need specification. Do not describe self reported completion as verified wellbeing. Never erase past achievements because a later month is hard.

Agreed early success signals: voluntary return and being able to explain what remains and what action comes next. The founder's longer term goal is building wealth from salary while clearing debt. Measure that aspiration separately from early usability and return; two pay cycles cannot establish a lasting wealth outcome.

Proposed activation: a reviewed saved plan and the ability to identify the next real action. Proposed return measure: people who return in the next eligible pay cycle to plan, record, adjust or review, divided by activated people whose next payday has occurred. Record reserve or goal contributions and remaining debt where the user has supplied enough information. A payment includes no assumed principal reduction when interest or fees are unknown. No verified net worth claim follows from a checklist. Numeric targets and clean sheet eligibility remain open (D07).

## Free boundary and premium concept (D08, D14)

**Agreed free boundary:** accurate income entry, basic planning, corrections and access to original records. The founder agrees to test one premium benefit at a time. Charging for basic multiple income entry would contradict this boundary; separate advanced views can be considered independently.

**Premium interaction agreed, 27 October 2026:** detect income from a connected account and use saved preferences to prepare an allocation draft for review. The user reviews before saving; the feature does not move money or automatically save the plan. Bank coverage, income detection rules, connection costs and data handling remain to be specified. Reusing saved rules after manual income entry is a proposed low cost pilot experiment, not an equivalent claim that a bank connection exists. A clickable simulation must be labelled as a simulation and must not request bank credentials.

Read access is a separate trust boundary from both manual entry and payment authority. A future connected flow needs an explicit data sharing choice, a way to disconnect, an explanation of data freshness, and a manual correction path. An imported credit must not automatically be classified as salary or new income when it could be a transfer between the user's own accounts. No live connection, provider choice or payment permission is approved by this concept discussion.

Primary source checked 27 October 2026: [Mono's product documentation](https://docs.mono.co/) distinguishes permission based financial data access from payments; its [account linking guide](https://docs.mono.co/docs/financial-data/connect-link) describes separate linking and data availability stages. This supports treating the requested bank connection as its own later design decision; it is not a provider recommendation or proof of real time salary detection.

**Payment sequence agreed, 27 October 2026:** free two pay cycle pilot, then a paid benefit test. Discuss the offer and price during the pilot; an actual paid offer needs one working premium benefit and explicit opt in. If the only valued benefit is bank connected automation and it is not available, do not charge for that promise. The report's price band remains a hypothesis; no billing date or price is set.

## Experience constraints and handoff

Design the mobile task first, with early checks at larger sizes. The research's roughly 90 second returning payday session is a hypothesis to measure, not a performance claim. Direct amount entry, visible gaps, readable numbers, accessible list interaction and reduced motion are required by the intended experience and project rules.

The installable web platform is already in the project direction. Any new requirement for native features needs a separate platform decision. Offline signed in money pages must not be cached in shared storage.

Before revising specs, resolve D01 through D07 and D12. Before choosing a paywall, resolve D08. Use the [validation plan](validation-plan.md) to gather evidence, then reconcile into the [existing scope](../scope/scope.md). This brief does not mark existing work complete or approve a model migration.
