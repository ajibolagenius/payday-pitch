The identity should be distinctly Nigerian with room to expand; a money word is not required in the name. Final name, palette, type and asset budget remain open (D09 to D11). Updated 27 October 2026.
**Sources:** Unified research [§2](../research/2026-10-26-unified-research-report.md#2-visual-direction), [§4](../research/2026-10-26-unified-research-report.md#4-brand-copy-type-logo-and-marketing), and the [naming report](../research/2026-10-26-unified-naming-report.md).

## Positioning

Proposed message: **Give your pay a game plan.** Plan on payday, follow through, and adjust when life changes. Your money stays with your bank or existing service.

Lead with the useful outcome, explain the formation, show a believable product example, and state the service boundary. Test whether this speaks to the first user's actual problem before producing campaigns.

Football is recorded product direction. The depth of football knowledge assumed by the brand remains open. The identity must help people understand salary planning without implying betting, borrowing, a bank account or guaranteed wealth.

**Geographic identity agreed, 27 October 2026:** distinctly Nigerian, with room to expand. Nigerian life, speech and obligations can inform examples and campaigns without making the brand exclusively Lagos based. This does not choose a Pidgin brand name, change the first market or approve every proposed code switch. Financial instructions remain clear and testable for the intended users.

**Naming constraint agreed, 27 October 2026:** the name does not need to mention pay or money; a descriptor can explain the service. No favourite or veto was supplied for the five candidates. Do not treat this answer as choosing Goal Difference or authorising a rename. The shortlist can retain names whose financial meaning comes from the descriptor.

Founder constraints still to establish: favourites and vetoes, company versus app naming, and willingness to investigate the Payday overlap. A money word is optional; the brand should feel Nigerian with room to expand, as agreed.

Use a common descriptor and a neutral demo. Hear each name before showing its spelling; ask what people think it does and what they remember later. Rotate presentation order. Do not show the report's ranking to participants.

Replace the report's sentence “I put my salary in my [name]” with **“I planned my salary with [name].”** The original prompt can itself suggest custody, which conflicts with the service boundary.

Compare hearing, spelling, understanding, unaided recall and false associations before preference. The report proposes rejecting a candidate if more than one or two of about ten people associate it with betting, loans or banking. Agree a fixed threshold before testing; this is qualitative screening, not statistical proof. Investigate any strong custody misunderstanding.

Then check finalists for current commercial availability, soundalikes and appropriate professional clearance. The report's DNS results dated 26 October are not proof a domain is available. A common word is not cleared by a domain search. No purchases or registration are included here.

## Voice and trust

Proposed voice: a clear, warm teammate. Football names moments; plain English describes actions. English carries amounts, errors and financial decisions. Optional Pidgin belongs in celebrations or campaigns and needs audience testing.

| Context            | Working copy                                                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup              | “What must this pay cover before your next payday?”                       |
| Save confirmation  | “Plan saved. Your money has not moved.”                                   |
| Checklist          | “Record as paid.”                                                         |
| Shortfall          | “Your plan is ₦20,000 over the pay you have recorded.”                    |
| Adjustment         | “Reassign ₦10,000 from Course fund to Food. Course fund becomes ₦65,000.” |
| Return after a gap | “Welcome back. Review your plan when you are ready.”                      |

Avoid “Move money” for an allocation edit. The report's approved verb “move” is ambiguous in a product that never transfers funds. Explain that money must be moved elsewhere when that is the real next action.

Reject guaranteed returns, risk free promises, betting idioms, shame, invented urgency and language implying the app holds money. Avoid “Sapa don clear!” after merely saving a plan: a plan does not establish that hardship has ended. Describe a clean sheet as recorded payment completion rather than verified financial health.

Keep the full candidate and tagline catalogues in the naming report. Final interface vocabulary belongs in the eventual revised design system, once the product model is agreed.

## Visual comparison, before choosing a system

**Founder preference recorded, 27 October 2026:** Matchday leads because of its colourful modules, with modifications welcome. Floodlight also appeals. The founder finds the other direction too editorial. This chooses the study direction, not a fixed palette, type family or default theme.

Develop the Matchday study around colourful modules, pitch inspired structure, readable money and expressive celebration moments. Marketing can be louder than amount editing or shortfall states. Warm paper, lime and ink remain candidate treatments to test, rather than every part of Matchday being approved together.

**Updated comparison recommendation:** with this week's minimal cost constraint, use Matchday as the lead and Floodlight as the focused challenger, with identical content. Modern Club Editorial remains in the research catalogue and can be revisited if evidence calls for it. This replaces the earlier recommendation to use Modern Club Editorial as the second study. A dark study does not approve a dark default or a change to the current production design rules.

Use the same plan task, adjustment and shortfall in every direction. Ask what the app does, what needs attention and what happens next before asking what looks best. Test icon recognition and betting associations separately. These are design studies; the existing [design rules](../../design.md) govern production until revised through scope and spec.

Colour roles remain distinct: identity, financial category, and status. Category colour alone must not communicate urgency or completion. Pair it with labels and symbols. Test actual text and control pairings in both themes, including large text and long amounts. Any prototype exception to current visual rules must be explicitly recorded as a study, not silently applied to the app.

## Type, logo and assets

The research favours Aspekta but reports missing ₦ and tabular figures in its inspected files. The current design already specifies a separate money face. **Proposed sequence:** decide whether Aspekta serves the chosen direction, inspect the relevant files and existing money face, then compare a verified numeral pairing with commissioning a renamed fork. A fork is an option, not a launch prerequisite by default.

Check the actual fonts with naira, aligned amount columns, negative values, easily confused characters, Nigerian names and 200% text size. Budget and rights remain open. Reference images of type are not licensed font files.

After naming, compare original formation dots, pitch letterforms or allocation bands. Do not commit to a P monogram if the name is unsettled. Check the mark at favicon and app icon sizes, in one colour, both themes, and for betting associations. No final logo or font purchase in this phase.

For the prototype, use only the assets needed to test comprehension. The report's four commissioned illustrations and full marketing kit are later spending choices. Choose the first growth channel after identifying where the first users already share plans or seek help. Cash referrals, paid creators and mini league promotion need their own value and budget decisions.
