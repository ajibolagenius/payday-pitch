**Date:** 26 October 2026  
**Type:** Fresh naming research (no reliance on existing product/code naming)

**Related research:** `docs/research/research-fresh-approach-2026-10-26.md` (product/visual direction; Matchday ritual, coach-not-casino voice).

## Naming brief (what a good name must do)

| Criterion                         | Why it matters                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sayable once**                  | WhatsApp / office / radio — if they mishear it, you lose growth.                                                              |
| **Spellable from hearing**        | App Store + Google; avoid cute misspellings unless you own the obvious domain.                                                |
| **2–3 syllables ideal**           | Fits app icon caption, wordmark, and “open ___” speech.                                                                       |
| **Sport + money without betting** | Must not read as odds, casino, tipster, or fantasy gambling.                                                                  |
| **Works for non-fans**            | Metaphor optional in the name; product can still use formation.                                                               |
| **Trust-safe**                    | Avoid regulated words you don’t hold: _bank, trust, insurance_ (and be careful with _capital, fund, invest_ as brand claims). |
| **Scales past v1**                | Should still fit if you add goals, seasons, education — not only “split calculator.”                                          |
| **Local-fit**                     | Easy for Nigerian English / Pidgin speakers; local-language roots welcome if tones/meaning are handled carefully.             |
| **Ownable**                       | Domain, social handles, trademark class 9 + 36 — finance is crowded.                                                          |

### Name shapes that work in fintech (research pattern)

1. **Coined short brands** — abstract, brandable (Zentari-style pattern).
2. **Evocative nouns** — imply safety, plan, or progress without saying “finance.”
3. **Soft compounds** — money/sport root + soft ending.
4. **Local-language meaning words** — e.g. Wekeza (“save/invest” in Swahili) as a category precedent.
5. **Ritual / moment names** — payday, matchday, kickoff as product moments.

### Collision notes already spotted (do not treat as cleared)

| Name / pattern                         | Issue                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **SquadPay**                           | Existing trip expense-splitting product (`squadpay.app`).                                                                                                         |
| **PitchPay**                           | Existing bill-payments product (`pitchpay.net`).                                                                                                                  |
| **MatchDayHQ / Formatio / TeamFeePay** | Grassroots club management / fee tools — sport+pay space is busy for _club_ money, not salary wealth.                                                             |
| **Àjọ / Esusu**                        | Powerful cultural savings words; also crowded conceptually with group-savings products and everyday speech — harder to own as a unique app brand unless stylized. |

**This list is creative research, not a trademark or domain clearance.** Shortlist → lawyer/TM search + domain/social check before lock.

## How the list is organized

Names are grouped by **naming strategy**. Each entry has a one-line rationale and a rough fit tag:

- **S** = sport-forward
- **M** = money/wealth-forward
- **B** = balanced metaphor
- **L** = local-language / Naija flavour
- **C** = coined / abstract

**Tier markers inside each group:**

- ★★★ — Strong for this concept (catchy + reasonable + on-brief)
- ★★ — Good alternate / worth testing
- ★ — Creative spice / riskier or narrower

## Scoring sheet (use in a 30-minute vote)

Rate each shortlist name 1–5:

1. Clarity of benefit without a pitch deck
2. Football cue without betting cue
3. Trust / premium for salary money
4. Ease of saying in Nigerian English / Pidgin
5. Likely ownability (gut)
6. Stretch to seasons/goals/education

**Disqualify immediately if:** sounds like betting, bank, crypto casino, kids’ game, or a club-subscription fee tool.

## Naming pitfalls specific to this product

1. **Fantasy football collision** — names that sound like FPL tools (_Draft, Wildcard, Bench Boost_) pull the wrong App Store neighbours.
2. **Club payments collision** — _PitchPay_, _SquadPay_, _MatchDay + pay_ patterns already used for grassroots fees.
3. **Gambling adjacency** — _odds, stake, accumulator, jackpot, lotto, casino_.
4. **Over-literal XI** — great for fans; test with people who “only watch AFCON finals.”
5. **Regulated vocabulary** — don’t imply banking/custody in the name unless that’s the product.
6. **Tone-mark landmines** — Yoruba money/wealth words change meaning with tone (_ajé_ vs _àjẹ́_).

## Suggested decision process

1. Pick **one primary lane**: Sport-forward (A), Finance-first (B), Coined (C), or Local (D).
2. Workshop-vote top **5** inside that lane.
3. Add **2 wildcards** from other lanes.
4. Run quick street tests: “What do you think this app does?” (n=10 salary earners, mix of football fans / non-fans).
5. Legal + domain + Instagram/TikTok/X handle check.
6. Lock **name + one-sentence positioning + banned associations** (betting, bank, guaranteed returns).

## Grill sheet (answer these to narrow the list)

1. **Must the word “pay,” “wage,” or “naira” appear in the name?** Or is metaphor-only OK?
2. **How football-native should the name be for someone who doesn’t watch football?** (Must understand / nice-to-have / irrelevant)
3. **English-only brand, or are you open to Yoruba/Pidgin as the primary name?**
4. **One word or two words allowed?** (Clean Sheet vs Matchpay)
5. **Do you want to sound closer to a sports media brand or a wealth app?**
6. **Any names you already love or refuse?** (Personal vetoes beat abstract research.)
7. **Will the company legal name and the consumer app name be the same?**
8. **Target domain:** `.app` / `.com` / `.ng` — which is non-negotiable?
9. **Are you OK educating the market for 6 months** (coined name) **or do you need instant comprehension?**
10. From shortlist A–D, **which lane feels like “us” on first gut feel?**
