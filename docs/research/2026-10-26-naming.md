# Payday Pitch — Naming Research Report

Date: 2026-10-26
Status: Research. No code changes. Companion to [2026-10-26-vision-refresh.md](2026-10-26-vision-refresh.md); it borrows that report's conclusions (Nigeria-first, formation core loop, trust constraint, anti-betting guardrail, consumer subscription).
Method: ~60 candidate names brainstormed from the football × money × Nigerian-Pidgin overlap, collision-checked by two research passes (Nigeria/Africa market + global fintech/trademark), then DNS-swept for `.com` / `.app` / `.ng` availability.
**Agent:** Qwen/Qoder

## 1. What makes a good name for this product

Criteria derived from the vision-refresh research, in priority order:

1. **Trust-safe** — nothing that reads payday-loan, betting, or scheme. ("Payday" carries loan-shark stigma in English; betting-brand collisions are disqualifying in Nigeria.)
2. **Ownable** — the formation category is empty, but many obvious football words are already squatted (Matchday = the FA's app + Messi-backed web3 game; Eleven = ElevenLabs; Keeper = Keeper Security; Starting XI / First Eleven = live football apps; Advantage = BofA's flagship banking line; Skipper = an Australian financial-wellness app; Regista = a Nigerian business app).
3. **Sayable in one try** — works on the phone, in church, at the barber: 2–3 syllables, phonetic spelling, no hyphens.
4. **Icon-legible** — fits the home-screen tile (≤ ~12 characters) and the wordmark (the "Matchday" visual system wants a strong lowercase word).
5. **Two-axis meaning** — the best candidates mean something in football _and_ something in money/life simultaneously (a "marking" double-meaning, a "goal difference" pun) — that's the brand in one word.
6. **Searchable** — a coined or unusual spelling beats a common noun for app-store and Google discovery.

### 2.5 Names checked and rejected (so nobody re-pitches them)

**Disqualified — betting collisions (the guardrail from the vision report applies to naming):**

- **Ginger** — Ginger Bet is an active Nigerian betting brand.
- **Jara** — BetJara (licensed NG betting) + JARA CASH loan app.

**Taken — direct or major-brand collisions:**

- **Keeper** (Keeper Security, 5M+ users) · **Matchday** (FA app, Messi-backed game, Matchday.com) · **Scoreline** (funded UK fantasy-football tech) · **Eleven** (ElevenLabs) · **Advantage** (BofA Advantage Banking) · **Skipper** (Skipper Money AU — financial wellness, direct sector) · **Captain** (Rapido/Uber-adjacent everywhere) · **Squad / SquadUp** (notorious in app circles) · **Regista** (Nigerian business app — home-market collision) · **Starting XI / First Eleven / FirstXI** (three live football apps + a $F11 token) · **No Wahala** (UBA's live loan product line) · **Oriki** (famous NG wellness group) · **Oba** (Oba Microfinance Bank + royal-title sensitivity) · **Kolo** (KoloPay, Kolomoni MFB — kolo-space is saturated) · **Gbedu** (funded Lagos music platform) · **Ajo / Esusu** (generic ROSCA words, fully claimed by apps) · **Golden Boot** (FIFA/Adidas award; active trademark filing culture around World Cup 2026) — RISKY.
- **Payday Pitch** itself: DNS-clean (com/app/ng all free) and no product collision found, but it inherits the **"payday" loan stigma** in English and is descriptive rather than ownable. It survives as a working title; it is not the recommended final name.

**Crowded — usable but weak IP or noisy neighbors:**

- **Formation** (legal-services category) · **Clean Sheet** · **Playmaker** (US Bank is actively marketing "Chief Financial Playmaker" — banks are circling this framing) · **Total Football** (Cruyff estate licensing) · **Tiki-Taka** (multiple tech users) · **Whistle** · **Terrace** · **Pitchside** · **Set Piece** · **Fixture** · **Build Up** · **The Gaffer** (a live football-tactics app already owns "Gaffer" — keep it as the _coach feature_ name, not the brand) · **Libero** (Italy's giant webmail) · **Three Points** (several small finance users) · **Dike / Eze / Sarki / N11** (saturated titles/surnames; N11 reads as "₦11" — a price tag, accidentally laughable).

## 3. Shortlist and recommendation

Ranked for this specific brief (Nigeria-first, premium-trust, gamified formation):

| Rank | Name                | One-line case                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1    | **Formashon**       | The category in one pidgin- coined word: zero collisions, all domains free, Fela lineage, street-legible, trademark-ownable, and the visual system ("your eleven, your formashon") writes itself. |
| 2    | **Money XI**        | The plain-spoken global option: says exactly what it is, XI is legally safe, all domains free, premium register.                                                                                  |
| 3    | **One Nil**         | The connoisseur option: the pragmatic-win philosophy _is_ the product ethos; short, elegant, ownable; .app free.                                                                                  |
| 4    | **Goal Difference** | The clever option: the money lesson (margin between in and out) smuggled inside football language; poster-scale wordmark.                                                                         |
| 5    | **Brollant**        | The Nigerian-heart option: pure pidgin for cash, nobody owns it, warm and memorable; risk = dated slang, harder for non-Nigerians.                                                                |
| 6    | **Salary XI**       | The safe descriptive option; slightly flat, maximum clarity.                                                                                                                                      |
| —    | **Ball Don Land**   | Not the brand — the tagline. Attach to any of the above: "Formashon — ball don land, shape your eleven."                                                                                          |
| —    | **Urgent 20**       | Not the brand — the emergency-fund ("Away Days") feature name.                                                                                                                                    |

**Recommended direction:** shortlist **Formashon, Money XI, One Nil** for the real test — icon-tile + wordmark mockups in the "Matchday" visual system, then the 5–10 target-user reaction test the vision report already proposed. Names die in the wild, not in lists: the deciding question is which one a user says to a colleague — _"I put it in my \___"_ — without embarrassment.

**Caveats:**

- DNS sweep = no nameservers found; it is a strong signal, not registrar truth. Verify final choice via WHOIS/registrar before committing.
- No formal trademark search was run (OG/NIPO for Nigeria, USPTO/EUIPO for global ambitions). Any finalist needs a registered trademark attorney search in at least Nigeria + classes 9/36 before launch.
- Betting-brand collision checks (Ginger Bet, BetJara) are current as of search date; re-check at launch.
