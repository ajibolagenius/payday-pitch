# 11. Matchday prototype and pilot preparation

**Date**: 2026-10-27
**Status**: In Progress

## Summary

Build an editable sample that tests whether you can see what remains after essentials and debt, make a plan, and follow it through. Use one colourful Matchday study, fictional figures and temporary state that resets on refresh. Prepare three candidate friends for comprehension sessions, then use the findings to define the later real pilot. The build budget is at most one working day, with no new spending.

## Requirements

**User stories**:

- As a participant, you can explore a sample before signup and explain what the app does with your money.
- As a planner, you can distinguish received money, forecast money, commitments, allocations and recorded actions in either pitch or list view.
- As the founder, you can run a repeatable session, record misunderstandings and decide what must change before a real pilot.

**Acceptance criteria**:

- **AC-1**: `/prototype/matchday` opens without an account, labels every screen as a fictional sample, and includes a simulated account step without credentials or account creation. Sample amounts are editable. Refresh, a fresh visit and a restored page reset the working state; a visible reset control does the same.
- **AC-2**: The builder supports Keeper, Defence, Midfield and Attack with real purpose labels and no required count of eleven. Pitch and list show the same amounts, warnings and actions, including direct amount editing. Required and extra debt repayments are distinct purposes. Keeper allows zero contribution and shows its opening balance separately.
- **AC-3**: Three sample salaries have separate expected and received amounts and dates. Late receipt retains the chosen coverage month unless explicitly reassigned. Received and forecast remainders subtract essentials and required debt; a separate left to assign figure subtracts all allocations. Opening carry forward is separate from income and reserve. The worked figures below reproduce exactly.
- **AC-4**: Review shows the overall funding gap, any essential funding gap and any underallocated required payment. Saving an underfunded sample requires explicit acknowledgement. Editing an allocation or income clears the acknowledgement. Saving never marks a payment complete or describes money as transferred.
- **AC-5**: Today supports recorded totals, partial payments, explicit zero and corrections. Unknown stays distinct from zero. The period report distinguishes incomplete information, unpaid obligations and a clean sheet. A correction recalculates only the affected period; optional contributions do not determine eligibility, and an empty plan earns no award.
- **AC-6**: An adjustment previews source, destination, amount and before/after values. Only known, uncompleted allocation is available. A completed extra repayment cannot fund a new food need. Applying the adjustment preserves the total allocation and cannot apply the same preview twice.
- **AC-7**: Food checks use Monday to Sunday weeks in Lagos time, clipped to the coverage month. Allowances sum exactly to the monthly food allocation. A remaining amount check does not imply a bank balance or verified payment. Unused allowance stays where it is until explicitly carried forward or reassigned; neither choice creates income.
- **AC-8**: Period review offers an explicit next month preview. It separates unused allocation from a declared amount still held, carries only a confirmed amount, and copies no received salary or recorded contribution as new income. Reserve and goal opening balances advance only from recorded contributions, never planned ones.
- **AC-9**: Short branches demonstrate one linked goal, all four reminder moments, a private share preview and complete, incomplete and unknown clean sheet states. Goal progress uses opening savings plus recorded contribution. Share preview contains only four line shape and line percentages, with no amounts, purpose names or personal identity. All reminders are previews, not scheduled notifications.
- **AC-10**: The single Matchday study uses existing fonts, artwork and accessible tokens. Amounts use `MoneyText`. All core actions work at 320px, at 200% text size and with a keyboard. Colour is supplemented by labels. Reduced motion removes animated movement. Pitch/list toggling preserves edits and focus remains usable after dialogs close.
- **AC-11**: The preparation pack records the three anonymous candidate profiles, known gaps and candidate only status. It includes recommended session owners, tasks, evidence fields and decision thresholds fixed before testing. It records no invitation, consent, result or enrolment that has not happened.
- **AC-12**: The pack distinguishes sample sessions from a later free pilot covering two actual pay cycles, defines activation and opportunity to return, and gives a gate for a later working paid benefit with explicit opt in. It claims neither live bank access nor money movement, and sets no unapproved price or billing date.
- **AC-13**: The prototype sends no entered values to the server, analytics or logs and writes none to browser storage, cookies or URLs. It adds no database tables, Server Actions, bank integration, email delivery, dependencies or credentials. Existing production routes, money rules and feature statuses retain their contracts.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: An isolated sample route in the existing Next.js application, using React page state and the existing UI primitives. Reuse the money and date modules, amount input, field artwork and vendored fonts. Use a new purpose based four line composition rather than adapting the existing fixed slot `PitchView`. (basis: feature 20, the confirmed founder answers, spec 0001, `design.md`, Ponytail.)

The runner up is a scripted clickthrough. It is faster, but cannot expose incorrect totals or adjustment behaviour when participants edit figures. A production schema migration would consume the validation budget before the model has been tested.

**Implementation skills**: Ponytail (`~/.gemini/config/plugins/ponytail/skills/ponytail/`), Secure Me (`~/.gemini/config/skills/secure-me/`), Impeccable (`~/.agents/skills/impeccable/`). These are installed skill locations, not new dependencies.

**Delivery**: Feature 20 retains Tracer Bullet and Beta. The first slice connects sample entry, a small plan, save, action recording and review before thickening that thread. `/architect` produces this specification; implementation and participant sessions remain future work.

## Feature design

### Surfaces and composition

Keep one public route with local screen state, under `src/app/(marketing)/prototype/matchday/page.tsx`. Use `src/features/matchday-prototype/` for the sample fixture and client UI. Read the applicable bundled Next.js guides before writing implementation code.

1. **Sample preview**: working name Payday Pitch, a plain money planning descriptor, the sample month, received/forecast distinction, pitch/list switch and “Explore the sample”.
2. **Simulated account step**: “In the real app you would create an account here. Continue with fictional figures.” No email or password fields and no link that silently enters live signup.
3. **Setup**: three editable sample incomes, actual receipt dates, coverage month, opening carry forward, separate reserve balance and purpose amounts. Keep the supplied purpose set; arbitrary purpose creation and a full debt ledger are outside this sample.
4. **Builder**: month, two headline remainders, secondary left to assign figure, four line groups, gap summary and direct purpose editing. Use the same editor from both views. Plain labels explain what the football lines mean.
5. **Review and saved**: amounts and funding warnings before a local save. Copy reads “Sample plan saved for this visit. Your money has not moved.” A saved intention does not complete checklist items.
6. **Today**: next due sample payment, checklist, food check and adjustment. Goal, reminder settings and share preview open short branches from here.
7. **Period report**: planned versus recorded, unknowns, clean sheet explanation and next month preview. Scenario controls explicitly simulate time rather than pretending the calendar has advanced.

Use colourful, labelled line modules within the existing token system. Preserve the installed type roles, including Tabular for money. Use existing semantic colours and tinted surfaces rather than adding a second palette. Keep financial errors visually distinct from category labels. A new layout provides the Matchday study; it does not rename the app, replace the production design system or approve a font purchase. Floodlight is deferred. (basis: brand brief, confirmed single study choice, `design.md`, Impeccable.)

### Temporary data model

These are TypeScript objects in page memory, not database tables. IDs are fixture strings unique within the sample; parent IDs must resolve. All amounts are nonnegative safe integers in kobo; derived gaps and remainders may be negative. Currency is fixed to `NGN`. All fields below are required unless marked nullable. Dates are valid `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, months `YYYY-MM`.

Fixture IDs, in fixture order: purposes `p-required-debt`, `p-transport`, `p-data`, `p-food`, `p-family`, `p-extra-debt`, `p-misc`, `p-emergency`, `p-course`; incomes `i-a`, `i-b`, `i-c`; plans `plan-2026-11` and `plan-2026-12`; goal `g-course`; food weeks `w-1` through `w-6`. Food's `plannedKobo` is never stored separately: it is always the sum of its week allowances. Editable purpose fields are `plannedKobo`, `recordedKobo` and `dueOn`. The `label`, `line`, `essential` and `requiredKobo` fields stay fixed in this sample. A `dueOn` outside the coverage month is allowed, labelled “Outside this month” and left out of that month's clean sheet. Each income has a coverage month choice of `2026-11` or `2026-12`; choosing December moves the row as described below.

Keep one active November plan and a bounded December draft for reassignment and next month preview. This uses two instances of the same Plan object, not a history feature. Reassignment moves an income row to the destination plan ID without copying it. The December draft begins with no incomes or purposes; its preview derives proposed openings from November and retains any income explicitly reassigned to December. It never imports November receipts automatically.

Required debt belongs in Defence. Extra debt repayment is an optional Midfield purpose. Essential priority is an explicit property, not inferred from a name or colour. The food purpose is essential but has no fixed payment due: its allowance is included in the essential budget, not treated as a bill that must be spent in full. The weekly remainder is displayed separately from checklist payment evidence.

The prototype uses one received total and date per income, sufficient for its three separate salary arrivals. Updating a partial receipt replaces that source's cumulative total and labels the date as the latest receipt date; it does not add the full salary again. An event history for several receipts from one source is a production follow up.

Recorded purpose amounts may exceed the plan, with an explicit warning; this never silently reduces another purpose or proves that money existed. Unknown recorded amounts are not assumed available for reassignment. A source with unknown completion first needs an explicit recorded amount, including confirmation of zero.

Dates and clipped week calculations belong in `src/domain/dates.ts` (the week helper is new; the module holds only period helpers today). At the prototype input boundary, remove commas before calling `parseNaira`, and pass `allowZero` for received, planned, recorded and remaining fields; production parsing stays unchanged. Use integer quotient and remainder allocation for exact distribution; do not introduce a second money utility.

### Food and period rules

November 2026 has six clipped food weeks: 1 November, 2 to 8, 9 to 15, 16 to 22, 23 to 29 and 30 November. With the ₦100,000 food plan, allocations are ₦3,333.34, ₦23,333.34, ₦23,333.33, ₦23,333.33, ₦23,333.33 and ₦3,333.33. They total exactly ₦100,000. This proration is a test assumption, not a permanent production rule.

Prorate only when the fixture is created. Thereafter the food purpose total is the sum of its week allowances. Its direct amount editor opens the week amounts, so editing the monthly plan cannot silently redistribute completed weeks. An adjustment into food names its destination week, defaulting to the week containing the scenario date. A food source uses a known week remainder, not `recordedKobo`; the food purpose itself has no editable payment record.

After a food check, preserve reported spending (`allowance - remaining`) when an adjustment changes the allowance: update allowance and known remaining by the same delta. Reject an allowance reduction below that spending. An unknown check stays unknown. Transfer only known remaining allowance, update both sides together, and leave it in the current week if no choice is made. Every week is editable at any clock date; the week containing the clock date is highlighted. The 30 November week has no next week, so carry to next week is unavailable there. A purpose destination for food money is any other purpose whose record is unknown or below its planned amount. At month end, unused allowance is shown for review; only an explicit declaration of money still held may become next month's opening carry. Reassigning to another purpose changes the food parent total and destination amount by equal opposite amounts. A transfer between food weeks leaves the food parent total unchanged.

A clean sheet needs at least one eligible fixed essential payment and a known recorded amount meeting every required payment due in the month. Missing records produce “Not enough information”; known unpaid amounts produce “Still due”. Show both counts if both exist. No eligible payments gives “No due payments”, not an award. Food is an essential budget, not a requirement to spend its whole allowance. Optional saving or repayment never disqualifies. This eligibility rule is a prototype test assumption built on the agreed due payment definition.

Correcting a recorded amount or eligible due date recalculates that report immediately. Explain a changed status without deleting unrelated months' achievements. The next month preview copies no actual income receipts or records, keeps any forecast explicitly forecast, and advances opening savings only from known recorded contributions. Reassigned December income is retained and labelled with its actual receipt date, since explicit reassignment is distinct from copying November's income into a new month. If a balance depends on an unknown contribution, label the balance incomplete and request confirmation rather than filling in zero. The sample previews December without building a season history store or another complete planning journey. The December preview shows only opening carry (once confirmed), opening reserve, goal opening savings and any income moved to December with its actual receipt date. It shows no purposes or remainders. The late receipt scenario demonstrates the move with income C.

State transitions are `sample preview → simulated account → setup → builder → review → saved → Today → period report → next month preview`. Navigation is reversible within the sample. Setup and Builder edits (incomes, planned amounts, coverage month) return the plan to draft and clear the shortfall acknowledgement. Adjustments and food transfers applied from Today keep the plan saved and leave the acknowledgement unchanged, because they preserve the total; if the after values would create or enlarge a funding warning, the preview names it and Apply needs a fresh acknowledgement. Checklist corrections update recorded evidence without converting the plan back into an unsaved allocation. A local save and an absolute amount edit are safe to repeat. Applying an adjustment or carry preview consumes that preview; a changed before value requires a fresh review.

### Extras and accessibility

The goal branch shows the target, opening savings, planned contribution, recorded contribution or unknown, and resulting known progress. For the sample goal, recording ₦75,000 changes known progress from ₦50,000 to ₦125,000; planning it alone does not.

Reminder examples cover expected payday, an upcoming essential, weekly food check and month review. All toggles start off. Example timing is one preview per event, as fixed text with no date computation, all at 18:00 Lagos time: “Tomorrow: salary A is expected. Record it when it arrives.”, “Tomorrow: transport is due.”, “Monday: check what is left of this week's food allowance.” and “30 November: review your month.” These are test assumptions, not a chosen production cadence.

Plain labels for the lines: Keeper is your reserve; Defence is essentials and required debt; Midfield is extra debt repayment and flexible spending; Attack is goals. No browser notification permission, email, push subscription or background timer is needed.

The share preview renders only generic line labels, shape and percentages. It has no hidden amount text, personal labels, identity, metadata or data attributes carrying those values. This milestone needs a preview, not image export or an actual share action. The share component receives only basis points and dot counts as props, so amounts never reach its DOM. Clean sheet celebration is one labelled badge that scales in with `motion/react`, gated by the reduced motion hook, and appears without movement under reduced motion.

Reuse the existing buttons, inputs, dialogs, `AmountField`, `MoneyText` and pitch field artwork. Keep text and controls readable with long amounts and large text; collapse to a vertical layout rather than shrink the pitch labels. Provide visible focus, labelled amount errors and a concise live summary when totals change. No action requires dragging, hover or colour recognition.

### Security and operational boundary

The public route exposes only fictional fixture data. No Supabase client, user query, real account form or persistent write is used. Temporary calculator style edits do not bypass the project's authenticated Server Action rule for persisted writes. No new environment variables, credentials or external tools are needed. (basis: `AGENTS.md`, spec 0001 public calculator convention, Secure Me.)

Keep entered state out of fetch requests, query strings, logs, analytics, localStorage, sessionStorage and cookies. The existing service worker may cache only the public initial sample shell; it receives no working state. Reset on a new document, explicit reset and a restored page event so browser restoration cannot retain a previous session's edits: a `pageshow` listener that sees `event.persisted` remounts the sample by changing its React key, which reloads the default fixture. No such listener exists in the repo yet. Keep entered values out of `console` output and error boundary messages. Signed in `/app` caching protections stay intact. Metadata uses noindex and nofollow; do not add the study to a sitemap or market it as a released feature.

Validation rejects malformed money, negative inputs, unsafe integers and any aggregate total above `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` kobo. Blank records remain null. Failed edits leave the last valid state usable and identify the field. There is no database retry, multiuser concurrency, pagination, transaction audit log or delivery failure to solve in this bounded sample. A future real data pilot needs those production decisions and the project's auth/RLS rules; the sample is not that implementation.
