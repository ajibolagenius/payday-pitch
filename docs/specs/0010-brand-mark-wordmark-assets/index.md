# 10. Brand mark, wordmark and identity assets

**Date**: 2026-10-25
**Status**: In Progress

## Summary

Replace the gold football mark with the formation system: dots on a chip that say _your money lined up_. The primary mark is eleven dots (gold outfielders, a light keeper alone at the base), the reduced mark is five plus the keeper for tiny sizes, and the wordmark `Payday Pitch` stays live Melodrama text plus one drawn SVG lockup for emails. A single row line-up asset starts the dot grammar. Everything ships through the existing icon pipeline, one token driven React component set, and the contrast test, with no manual PNG edits anywhere.

## Requirements

**User stories**:

- As a person scanning browser tabs and a home screen, I want a mark that reads as "my money lined up" so that the product is recognizable even at 16 pixels.
- As a developer building later slices, I want one component set and one icon pipeline for all brand art so that the identity is never pasted as loose pictures.
- As the product owner, I want the mark, wordmark and lockup to hold up alone and together, in both themes, with the contrast test proving it.

**Acceptance criteria**:

- **AC-1**: The primary mark reads as a 3 / 3 / 4 / 1 formation at 512, 192, 64 and 48 px, and the reduced mark (gold rows of 3 and 2 with one light keeper alone at the base) is the art used at 32 and 16 px. Every size is checked as a real rasterization from a generated file or rendered frame, not a zoomed SVG.
- **AC-2**: The maskable master scales its formation to 0.72 or smaller on the full bleed forest chip, verified by the launcher circle simulation (radius 156 on 512; the keeper, the deepest element, stays within it).
- **AC-3**: `pnpm icons` regenerates the whole set from the new masters with no manual PNG edits: `public/icons/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `src/app/apple-icon.png` from the primary master, `public/icons/icon-maskable-512.png` from the maskable master, `src/app/icon.png` (the favicon source) from a new reduced master.
- **AC-4**: No hex literal enters `src/components/brand/**`: the components paint from raw ramp variables (`--gold-200`, `--gold-600`, forest, paper), declared as artwork reading layer 1 as spec 0009 allows, and `src/lib/brand-palette.ts` gains exactly one literal, `gold: "#F0C75E"`, for the emails to read. `pnpm check:hex` passes with no new exclusion added for the brand directory.
- **AC-5**: The wordmark renders as live text in Melodrama 500 or 600, tight tracking, sentence case, through the already vendored variable font (no fallback flash), with Switzer for any supporting line and Teko never in the wordmark.
- **AC-6**: Two drawn assets ship under `public/brand/`: the horizontal lockup (primary chip at the left, `Payday Pitch` to its right, no divider) and the line-up (eleven dots in one row at equal spacing, keeper leading left). The lockup is generated by `scripts/render-lockup.mts`, which traces the vendored Melodrama woff2 at the pinned dimensions in Feature design, so it regenerates rather than drifts. Both appear on the `/design-system` page and the lockup is the email brand block in the auth emails.
- **AC-7**: Both tiers exist as a forest chip (gold 200 dots, paper keeper) for light surfaces and a paper chip (gold 600 dots, forest keeper) for dark surfaces. The pair contract in `src/lib/tokens.ts` gains these four pairs, each at 3:1 or better against its own chip (WCAG 1.4.11, graphic objects): gold 200 on forest, paper on forest, gold 600 on paper, forest on paper.
- **AC-8**: The `/design-system` page shows both tiers, both chip variants, at 512, 192, 64, 48, 32 and 16 px, with the maskable crop simulation drawn next to the maskable render.
- **AC-9**: The old ball is gone everywhere: `public/icons/master*.svg` hold the formation art, the emails, manifest, offline page and marketing placeholder carry the new mark, and a repo grep for the old seam path data returns nothing.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 2: Direct replacement in one slice

Build the formation identity as one system: three masters feeding `pnpm icons`, a raw ramp driven component set in `src/components/brand/`, a script generated lockup and a static line-up asset, both chip variants pinned to values the pair contract can prove, and one branch that ends when the old ball greps up nowhere.

**Implementation skills**: `sharp` (`.agents/skills/sharp/`, master to PNG regeneration behind `pnpm icons`) · `impeccable` (global, `~/.agents/skills/impeccable/`, governs surface and asset work) · `vitest` (`.claude/skills/vitest/`, pair contract extension) · `playwright-cli` (raster proof of the brand grid and the crop simulation)

## Feature design

**Geometry (canonical and complete; no build step may read `docs/assets/brand-candidate/`, which is gitignored scratch)**:

- Primary mark, 512 box: forest chip `rx=112`; ten gold 200 dots `r=23` in a row of 3 at cy 118 (cx 136 / 256 / 376), a row of 3 at cy 220 (cx 172 / 256 / 340) and a row of 4 at cy 322 (cx 100 / 206 / 306 / 412); keeper `r=31` at cx 256, cy 436 in paper. Row gap within the formation is 102; the keeper gap is 114, a deliberate optical breathing room for the larger dot (recording the exception to the scope's equal gaps rule).
- Reduced mark, 512 box: gold row of 3 at cy 160 (cx 136 / 256 / 376, `r=32`), gold row of 2 at cy 292 (cx 188 / 324, `r=32`); keeper `r=42` at cx 256, cy 424.
- Maskable master: the primary formation scaled 0.72 about the center on a full bleed forest square (no corner radius needed under maskable).
- Line-up asset, 512 by 144: forest band; gold dots `r=15` at 37 px steps from cx 108 to cx 441, keeper `r=21` leading at cx 71, all cy 72.
- Lockup, 736 by 160 canvas: primary chip scaled to a 128 box at y 16; 28 px gap; `Payday Pitch` set in Melodrama 600, cap height 72, sitting on baseline y 116, sentence case, tight tracking matching the live setting.

**Component surface** (`src/components/brand/`, artwork conventions, raw ramp variables only, no hex):

- `<BrandMark tier="primary" | "reduced" chip="forest" | "paper" />` renders the inline SVG from the canonical geometry. Forest chip paints gold 200 dots and a paper keeper; paper chip paints gold 600 dots and a forest keeper. All four colors are raw layer variables, so a theme switch is a prop or a token read, not a second file.
- `<Wordmark />` live Melodrama text, sentence case, tracking set from `design.md` rules.
- `<Lineup />` the eleven in a row, for the showcase now and dividers later.
- Sizes are props: the primary tier renders at 48 px and above, the reduced tier is mandatory below 48.

**Email path**: emails always take the forest chip (email is a light surface). `src/lib/brand-palette.ts` gains `gold: "#F0C75E"` (forest, ink, paper and card already exist there). The auth and recovery emails inline the reduced mark (footer) and the lockup (header) as JSX that interpolates `BRAND` constants only; pasting the raw SVG files into `src/emails/**` is rejected because their hex fills would fail `pnpm check:hex` (react-email skill conventions apply).

**Key invariants**:

- One geometry source per tier: component and master SVGs share the coordinates above; changing the formation means editing both in the same commit.
- No hex outside the raw layer, the carve out, and `brand-palette.ts` (`pnpm check:hex` gate; brand components stay clean by reading variables).
- Below 48 px only the reduced tier renders; the primary tier never sizes under 48.
- `pnpm icons` and `scripts/render-lockup.mts` are the only ways PNGs and the lockup change; a hand edited generated file is a review reject.

**Critical test scenarios**:

- Happy path: `pnpm icons` writes every output, and a Playwright brand grid spec screenshots `/design-system` at 512, 192, 64, 48, 32 and 16 px; each frame reads as the formation with the keeper visible, verifies **AC-1**, **AC-3**.
- Geometry edge: the maskable render overlaid with the radius 156 crop circle shows no dot cut, verifies **AC-2**.
- Contrast: the Vitest pair contract holds the four mark pairs at 3:1 or better in both themes, verifies **AC-7**.
- Literal hygiene: `pnpm check:hex` passes with `src/components/brand/**` included in the scan (no exclusion added), verifies **AC-4**.
- Grep gate: the old seam path data and ball assets return nothing; emails and offline page reference only new brand parts, verifies **AC-9**.
- Wordmark: marketing and auth surfaces show Melodrama sentence case from the vendored variable font (no fallback flash in a rendered frame), and the generated lockup matches those settings, verifies **AC-5**, **AC-6**.

## Migration plan

**Strategy**: direct replacement (single branch, no live users)
**Phases**:

1. Masters and pipeline land; every generated PNG regenerates in the same commit.
2. Components, generated lockup and emails switch; the ball files delete in that commit, not after.
   **Rollback**: revert the branch merge; one commit set restores old masters, script jobs and surfaces together.
   **Risks**: the lockup generator depends on a new dev dependency that could bite on a fresh install (mitigation: pinned version, script fails loudly); dark theme surfaces choose the wrong tier at small sizes (mitigation: the surface map and invariant plus review).

## Consequences

**Positive**:

- One brand system instead of one icon: the dot grammar has a home (`src/components/brand/`) that features 15, 16 and 18 will reuse without redesign.
- Favicon sizes finally carry meaning instead of noise.
- Every raster and the lockup are reproducible from committed sources, so the identity survives any reinstall.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:

- Two tiers, two chip variants, plus lockup and line-up: several geometries to keep in sync, guarded by one shared coordinates rule and the surface map rather than by code.
- The paper chip necessarily reads darker and more muted than the forest chip (gold 600 is a brown gold on paper), a cost of the 3:1 floor; the brand looks slightly different between themes by design.
- Inline SVG in emails grows their payload slightly, and their colors are literals that must be edited by hand if the palette moves again.
- Two new dev dependencies (`wawoff2`, `fontkit`) for lockup regeneration.

**Neutral**:

- `pnpm check:hex` and the icon pipeline gain files but no new rules; no carve out expansion is needed because brand components read variables.
- This branch sits on spec 0009's tip, so 0009's verify run should cover both changes after merge.
